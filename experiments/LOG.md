# Experiment log

Every post, every variation, every result. Public on purpose — running experiments in the open is
itself the content, and nobody else in this lane does it.

## The two rules

**One variable at a time.** Same artifact, same destination, same platform. Only the first line
changes. Change two things and this log tells you nothing, forever.

**Reply rate, never impressions.** 2,000 views with 9 replies beats 50,000 with silence. Replies are
a list of people you can talk to. Views are a number.

## When to stop testing

| Signal | Do |
|---|---|
| One hook at 3x+ the reply rate, over 5+ posts | **Stop testing. Write 10 more of that one** |
| All hooks flat and low | The artifact is wrong, not the hook. Go back a step |
| A hook wins once | Not a winner. One post is noise |
| A winner fades after 10+ posts | Saturation in that room. Same hook, new room |

The failure mode is abandoning a working angle out of boredom. **You will be sick of the winning
hook long before your audience has seen it.**
