portfolio-from-work

What this cannot do

✕It will not remember you. Every session starts from zero. You re-explain your product each time.
✕It cannot tell you if the output is right. It produces. It does not check.
✕It does not improve because you used it. Your corrections die when the session closes.

Those are the difference between a folder of skills and a system. They are stated here because you will hit them by day three, and they are not bugs. This repo is genuinely yours under MIT either way.

Get each new skill the day it ships

One new skill lands in this repo every week. Drop your email and you get it the day it ships — plus the eval golden sets: the frozen adversarial inputs each skill is tested against, with the traps documented. That is how you check whether a skill actually works instead of just producing output.

No newsletter. No drip sequence. A skill, when there is a skill.
