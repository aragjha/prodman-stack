# 03 · Accountants — DRAFT

Channel: "What's new" panel in the app only. ~1,800 seats.
Most read just the headline and the first line, so the headline must carry the message. No jargon,
no ticket numbers, no mention of the API (most don't know what one is).

---

## What's new panel

**Chase overdue clients faster, and send invoices in bulk**

- **Filter your client list to clients with overdue invoices** — find who to chase in one click.
- **Send up to 50 draft invoices at once** instead of one at a time.
- **You'll now get an email when a client pays an invoice** you own. Don't want it? Turn it off in
  Settings → Notifications.
- **Export payments to CSV for any date range** from Reports. *(A very large export can time out for
  now — export a shorter date range.)*
- **Fixed:** due dates no longer show a day early in some time zones.

*Not shown to accountants: the new-firm default template (new firms only) and the API change (not
