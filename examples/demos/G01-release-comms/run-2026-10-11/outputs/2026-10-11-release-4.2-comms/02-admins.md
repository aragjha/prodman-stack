# 02 · Firm admins — DRAFT

Channels: monthly product email + "What's new" panel in the app.
Audience: 1–2 per firm, ~300 firms. They care about billing, permissions, settings and what their
team sees, and want to know if they need to do anything. No ticket numbers (house style).

---

## Subject: What's new in Ledgerly — October 2026

Hi there,

Here's what changed in this month's release. One item needs a quick decision from you; the rest
just work.

**You may want to act on this one**

- **Your team now gets an email when a client pays an invoice.** When a client pays, the accountant
  who owns that client is emailed automatically. **This is on by default.** If you'd rather your
  team not receive it, each person can turn it off in **Settings → Notifications**.

**Faster daily work (no action needed)**

- **Send up to 50 draft invoices at once** from the invoice list, instead of one at a time.
- **Filter your client list to just the clients with overdue invoices**, so chasing is quicker.
- **Export payments to CSV for any date range** from Reports.
  *Known issue:* a very large export (more than 10,000 rows) can time out. For now, export a shorter
  date range. A fix is planned for the next update.

**Fixed**

- Due dates no longer show a day early for firms in some time zones.

That's it. Nothing here changes your billing or your permissions.

— The Ledgerly team
