# Who reads Ledgerly release notes

## Firm admins
- One or two per firm, across about 300 paying firms.
- Read the monthly product email and the "What's new" panel in the app.
- Care about anything that changes billing, permissions, settings or what their team sees.
- Want to know whether they need to do anything.

## Accountants
- Most of our roughly 1,800 seats.
- See only the "What's new" panel in the app, and most read just the headline and the first line.
- Care about their daily invoicing work: what is faster, what is fixed, what moved.
- No jargon. Most of them do not know what an API is.

## Our API partner
- One partner: a document management product used by about 40 of our firms. Their integration reads invoices and payments from our v2 API every night.
- Readers are their engineers. Our contact is Jonas, their integration lead.
- They read our API changelog and any email we send Jonas directly.
- They need exact field names, before and after examples, the date a change goes live, and what they must change on their side.
- Our partner agreement says we give at least 14 days' written notice before any breaking API change.

## House style
- Plain words. Say what changed and what the reader should do about it.
- No ticket numbers in anything firm admins or accountants see.
