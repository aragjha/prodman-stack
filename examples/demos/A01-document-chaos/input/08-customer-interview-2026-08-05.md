# Customer interview: office manager, 9 seat firm (account 573)
Interviewer: Lena (design lead). Notes: Sam (designer)
Date: 5 August 2026. Length: 35 minutes.
Context: 9 seat firm, about 60 retainer clients, on Ledgerly since 2024.

## Quotes (verbatim)
- How they bill today: "On the first of the month I copy last month's invoice for every retainer client. That's my whole morning, sixty times."
- Schedules: "Everyone is billed on the first. Four or five clients are quarterly. That's it."
- Pausing: "A couple of clients stop their retainer over the summer, and then I have to remember to start them again."
- Checking: "I'd still want to look at them before they go out. One wrong invoice to a client is embarrassing."
- Late payers: "Every Friday afternoon I go down the list of who hasn't paid and email them one by one. Some of them I email three Fridays in a row."
- On automation: "If Ledgerly chased them for me, I'd move our two new juniors onto Ledgerly instead of giving them the spreadsheet."
- Control: "I wouldn't want it emailing our biggest client a nasty reminder. I need to say who gets chased."
- Autopay: "A few clients would love paying automatically. Most would never give us their card."

## Observations (Lena)
- Copying retainer invoices and chasing late payers both came up without prompting. Chasing came up first and took most of the call.
- She keeps a paper list of who to chase next to her keyboard.
