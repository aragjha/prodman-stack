# Recurring invoices: requirements list (merged, not deduplicated)
Compiled by: Noor (frontend engineer)
Date: 19 August 2026
Sources: sticky notes from the 18 August workshop, plus a copy of Lena's list from July

## Workshop list
1. Invoices for retainer clients go out by themselves every month
2. Change the retainer fee for future months without touching invoices already sent
3. Put a client's billing on hold and switch it back on later
4. Billing forecast screen: what will be invoiced over the next two months
5. The accountant gets a heads up before an automatic invoice goes out
6. Custom recurrence rules (every second Tuesday, last working day of the month)
7. Charge the client's card automatically when the invoice is issued
8. Editing a series must never change invoices that were already sent
9. Recurring invoices in EUR as well as USD
10. Remind clients automatically when an invoice is overdue
11. Suspend a client's series while they are on a payment plan, then restart it

## Lena's list (copied from July)
- Auto send on the billing date
- Hold and resume
- Preview before send
