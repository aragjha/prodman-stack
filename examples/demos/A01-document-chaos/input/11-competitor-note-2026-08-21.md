# Competitor note: Falcon (code name)
From: Rosa (head of sales)
To: Maya, Daniel
Date: 21 August 2026

## What changed
- Falcon's August release added automatic payment reminders: up to three reminder emails per overdue invoice. The firm picks the timing and the wording, and can switch reminders off for any client.
- Reminders are included in their base plan, no add on.
- Their public changelog still shows no recurring invoices.

## Where we meet them
- Falcon came up in 4 of our deals since 1 July. We lost 2 of those 4, both after the prospect asked about reminders.
- In the other 2 we won on bank feeds and price.

## What I think
- Reminders are now table stakes in any deal where Falcon shows up.
- Recurring invoices is still our edge, because they don't have it.
- If we ship recurring invoices without reminders, I expect we keep losing the deals where the buyer's main pain is chasing.
