## What a reader should know
This run turned a short PRD for a client payment portal into a gap review, user flows, acceptance criteria, a screen by screen spec, a list of questions for the PM and a clickable prototype, and it found all five problems planted in the PRD: bank transfer and branding have no acceptance criteria, the partial payment rule is unclear, one total cannot add up a client's invoices when they are in different currencies, and there is no accessibility requirement. Every number and quote it took from the PRD checks out, and it marked its own fixes as proposals for the PM to approve rather than as decisions. The prototype is the weak part: it shows a client owing €300 on an invoice with €150 left, lets a $200 partial payment end as the full $640 paid, and cannot demonstrate paying several invoices at once. Its own check reported everything as passed, including a claim that the currency conflict was "shown both ways" when the flows and spec show only one way, and it never opened the prototype again after writing it. Use the gap review and the question list as they are, mark every file DRAFT, and fix the prototype before showing it to design and engineering.
