## The single worst problem
The plan never tests its riskiest assumption before committing. The run rightly says the seat link rests on one customer sentence. It then puts both squads to work from January and sets its only check, "by mid-Feb 2027, if <25% of firms that enable reminders add ≥1 seat within 30 days", after a reminders build it plans to "target ship end Feb 2027". Even at its fastest sizing, 6 weeks from Monday 4 January, reminders ship on 15 February, and a 30 day window closes on 17 March. From the end of February, the window closes on 30 March, one day before the deadline. So about 93 engineer weeks (3.5 plus 4 engineers over 12.4 weeks) are spent before the assumption can fail, and the reader is promised a mid February kill switch that cannot fire.

## Smaller problems
- "that is literally what customer Q1 offered to do" overstates line 28. She offered to move two juniors onto Ledgerly if it sent her Friday emails; she did not offer to end a "manual spreadsheet chase".
- "Squad A's payments/bank familiarity" is not in idea.md. Line 22 only says Squad A spends about 30% of its time on bank feed maintenance.
- The request asked how competitors charge, but the run never priced the payment step. Yet the FreshBooks page it cites lists card and bank transfer fees and a paid "Advanced Payments ($20/mo)" extra. That undercuts the report's line "Ledgerly cannot sell them as a paid add-on".
- MRR and ARR are never explained in plain words, which the skill's plain language rule requires.

## What the run did well (one line)
It caught the numeric traps and the main requirement: 80 seats last quarter against a 300 seat target (3.75 times), Squad A at 3.5 engineers, the portal scoped to the fewer than 50 firms with card payments on, firm control over reminders as a launch requirement, and all three customer quotes kept word for word.

## What a reader should know
The roadmap gets the arithmetic and the team limits right, but its check on the big assumption, that reminders will make firms buy more seats, cannot work as written. Reminders are planned to ship at the end of February, so a 30 day count of new seats cannot report by mid February, and both squads are committed for the whole quarter before anything is tested. Its headline that reminders and a client payment portal are free in every major rival goes further than its sources: reminders are standard in the four tools it checked, but it shows a client portal only for FreshBooks, and the pages it cites show rivals charging for the payment itself. A Bill.com quote and three Bill.com prices are not on the page they cite, and the run's own quote checker reported clean without comparing a single quote with a web page. Before acting on this plan, ask for a cheap test of the seat assumption, with a pass mark, that can finish before both squads commit.
