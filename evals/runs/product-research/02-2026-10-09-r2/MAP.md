**Belief:** **confirmed on the half that was testable, unknown on the half that was not.** (1) The pain B names is real and recognised in the lane — a five-source pattern (S12, S13, S14, S15, S16) with 580 likes on the loudest instance, and two PMs saying it in their own words (S3, S4). (2) The free-repo lane for B is empty — nothing above 5 stars, closest competitor 4 stars (S20, S21) — while A is owned by a 7,209-star incumbent that already uses A's exact words (S18, S19). (3) PMs convert on the eval framing elsewhere: 2,653 free signups (S23) and 5,000+ at $4,200 (S22). What the map cannot say is that B **converts better** on *this* page: no head-to-head exists (S29), and the only pain-vs-capability number is from another domain (S28). "Names a pain the other doesn't" — confirmed. "Converts better" — the hypothesis week one tests.

**Falsifier:** did not fire. (i) Two PMs describe evals as their problem in their own words (S3, S4) — a weak signal, both prompted, with Reddit unreachable (S7). (ii) No free repo owns the angle: 4 stars is the ceiling (S20).

**Contradicting evidence:** S11 — a PM's unprompted AI pain was organisational, not evals; B reaches only the sub-segment shipping AI features (S2 unknown). S18 — angle A has demonstrated pull (7,209 stars) and B has zero track record on GitHub; stars are not emails, but A is not "nobody wants it". S9 — HN silence on evals-for-PMs (Hamel's canonical post: 1 point, 0 comments), adjacent because HN is an engineer room. Weak confirms, cited because they are weak: S3 and S4 are prompted, not unprompted; S10 is vendor-reported; S15 and S16 are low-engagement or unattributed; S27 says exactly why prompted words count for less. Adjacent: S6 (role unknown), S28 (health domain, 2010). Could not verify: S7 (Reddit). Nothing found: S8 (Quora), S25, S29, S30.

**Top three, by impact on the goal (50 signups):**
1. **The B lane is empty and the A lane is owned** (S18, S19, S20, S21). On Dunford's structure (S26), A's competitive alternative is a free 7,209-star repo — a page positioned there competes for signups against something better-known and free. B's alternatives are $4,200 or 4 stars.
2. **The own-words pain is "complex, hard to implement, non-technical" (S3, S21), not "I lack evals" (S4).** Copy that says "evals without becoming a data scientist" matches the words found; copy that says "the eval layer" assumes a term one PM had "never even heard" (S4).
3. **2,653 PMs signed up for a free "AI Evals for Product Managers" lesson** (S23) — the nearest observed conversion on B's headline family. With a 1M-subscriber host; the user's page will not have that host, which is why S1/S2 matter for whether 50 is reachable.

**Next step:** **decide** — ship B, worded in the lane's own words ("evals for PMs shipping AI features, without becoming a data scientist"), and treat the week-one 50 as the conversion test the map could not run. The decision does not reverse on any number the map lacks: S1/S2 change whether 50 is hit, not which headline; S7 (Reddit) could only add own-words evidence, and the falsifier already failed to fire without it.
