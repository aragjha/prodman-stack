## Observations for the author

1. **Run-01's gap is not operationally one unknown.** It asks for "how many would pay a stated ₹/$ per seat" while forbidding "would you" questions and listing Mom-Test prompts (what happened after the last meeting, how notes are handled, what it cost) that cannot produce a take-rate. And the reversal thresholds ("3% funds sync; 30% funds notes") assume the sync side is sized, which row 1.2 says it is not. Either name the take-rate and accept it is a price test, or name S2 (sync revenue-at-risk) — not both wearing one label.
2. **Run-03's verdict quietly re-targets the decision.** The user's decision is build / narrower slice / hold; the named gap decides slice shape. Row 1.1 already concedes the stated decision needs S1. The honest step was "one named gap: the goal number" (or S1-plus-nothing), with the schema eval as the *first action* after it.
3. **RESPONSE.md re-opens gaps after a verdict in four of five runs.** Run-01: adds the Reddit re-run and S1/S2 ("without them neither option is sized") — mirrors the verdict, so the FAIL is the verdict's. Run-02: after a decide, lists Reddit (S7) and "two numbers only you hold and the map needs before the 50 means anything" (S1, S2). Run-04: after one named gap, adds FIU status as the thing that "sets the pre-round timeline" — the row-7.3 "named sub-gap" that the verdict kept out. Run-05: adds two verification items (S15, S29). Only run-03's response is consistent with its verdict — in the wrong direction (two gaps both places).
4. **Run-02's map argues against its own verdict in one row.** Row 1.2 "Also changes": "if it is small, B's ceiling is small even if its rate is higher" — for a goal of 50 signups, a ceiling is a headline-relevant quantity. The angle-1 insight and the verdict both say S2 changes "whether 50 is hit, not which headline". Pick one; the row as written is the sentence a sceptic quotes back.
5. **Run-02's ground truth is not in its REQUEST.md.** The decision (A vs B), belief, falsifier and "50 in the first week (user's number)" appear nowhere in the request in the folder, which asks for a market overview. If that came from the run's one question, the answer is not in the folder, so a reviewer cannot confirm the "decide" is measured against the user's number. Not an R8 failure; a provenance hole.
6. **The Reddit archive lane was blocked in all five runs** (S16, S7, S20, S14, S21 — 429 / 422 / blocked) and three verdicts hand it back to the user as something to re-run. A lane that fails every run is a tooling defect to fix at the skill, not a gap to assign.
