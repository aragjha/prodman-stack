## What the author should hear

1. **"counter-signal" is the systemic leak, and it is not an equivalent of "contradicts".** All five runs use it; all five verdict sections list it as a category *separate from* contradicts / weak confirm / adjacent; run-01's verdict literally labels the group `counter-signals on the build`. In run-01 it means "weighs against building notes", in run-04 it means "weighs against the contradicting cases" (i.e. mildly *for* the belief), in run-05 it means "weighs against shipping scores". A word whose direction relative to the belief flips between runs is a build/decision consequence wearing a belief-column costume. Fix: replace every `counter-signal` with `contradicts (weak)`, `confirms (weak)` or `unknown`, and move the "against the build" sentence to Also changes, where it belongs.

2. **Rows that do not bear on the belief must say `unknown`, not borrow a direction.** Run-01 angle 7 is the cleanest proof: the Insight says `unknown (constrains the how, not the want)` while rows 7.1 and 7.2 above it say `counter-signal`. Same for run-01 row 3.10 (Calendly's sync bug says nothing about whether ops leads want notes) and run-03 rows 4.1 / 4.4 / 7.4 (an archived repo, a dormant repo, a permission leak — none tests "data teams want it"). When the author knows the finding constrains the *how*, the Belief cell is `unknown`; the consequence goes in Also changes.

3. **Dual verdicts (run-03) are honest but violate "exactly one".** Seven run-03 rows split the compound belief into halves inside one cell (`confirms (demand) · contradicts (reliability)`). Either name the half in the row's Finding text and give one verdict against it, or split the belief in §0 into two numbered beliefs and mark rows against B1 / B2. The Insight lines may legitimately carry two verdicts; the rows may not.

4. **"adjacent" and "trust" are marks, not verdicts.** Run-02 row 3.3 and the pattern `confirms — trust: vendor CEO` (run-03 2.2) show the Mark column leaking into Belief. Keep adjacency and trust in Mark (HIGH / MEDIUM / adjacent) and keep Belief to the three words.

5. **Insight lines passed everywhere.** All 37 Insight lines carry `**Belief:**` followed by confirms / contradicts / unknown (sometimes qualified, which is fine). Run-03 angle 7's reads `contradicts the falsifier's "accuracy failures" clause is live` — a dropped dash, not a criterion failure; worth a one-character fix.

6. **Severity order, for triage:** run-01 (9 rows, verdict wording proves the leak) > run-03 (literal `also changes:` in the column, 3 bare counter-signals, 7 dual rows) > run-05 (2 bare counter-signals) ≈ run-02 (1 bare `adjacent`, 2 counter-signals that at least carry a direction in parentheses) > run-04 (1 ambiguous row; otherwise the model answer for this criterion).
