## Observations for the author

1. **The failure pattern is one pattern, in all three maps.** When a finding bears on the decision, the build, the method or the next step rather than on the belief, the column "Changes the belief?" gets *that* consequence written into it, and the belief is never mentioned. Run-04's Angle 7 and Angle 8 Insight lines show the fix in six words: "**Impact:** no change to the belief; sets non-negotiables for a spec." / "**Impact:** none on the belief." Say "none" or "unknown" first; then add the decision consequence after a semicolon. Run-05 row 2.4 does exactly this; row 4.5, written minutes later, forgets.

2. **Severity is not equal.** Run-01's miss is structural: two whole angles (7 Constraints, 8 Distribution) contain nothing — no row, no Insight — that says what they do to the belief, and seven more rows in angles 4–6 do the same. Run-04 and run-05 each miss on one or two substantive rows and would pass with a word apiece. They are failed because the criterion says *every* row; the author should read the three verdicts as "one structural, two cosmetic", not as three equal failures.

3. **Null results need a rule.** Run-04 handles "nothing found" three ways in one file: "**unknown** (absence in opened pages)", "**does not confirm**", and "—". Run-01 uses "neutral", "not counted" and "**F1 not resolved**". Pick one form for a null row — "**unknown** — not found" costs nothing and satisfies the column — and one form for ZERO rows ("not counted" is fine, it is explicit). The dash is the only form that fails, because it says nothing.

4. **Angle 1 (Goal) has no Insight line in run-04 or run-05**, and run-05's Angle 1 paragraph never names the belief. R6 had nothing to check there, but a closing "**Impact:** unknown — the goal number is yours to supply" would make the angle consistent with the other seven and stop a stricter reviewer flagging the gap.

5. **Run-01's angles 5–8 were run "lightly" and the verdict says it does not depend on them** — which is exactly where the belief-impact discipline collapsed. If an angle is run only to set cost or find rooms, the honest column entry is "no change to the belief — sets X"; running it lightly is no reason to drop the statement.

6. **The two question-only runs are the two cleanest runs.** Both refuse for the right reason (no belief → nothing to confirm or contradict → no R6-checkable map), both ask once, both offer the user a way to short-circuit. Run-03 additionally refuses to drop the contradicting section while still giving the user control of the slide — that is the right line and it is drawn plainly in the first paragraph.
