# R8 — a step a decision can take

Criterion: the MAP.md verdict (section 3) must end in exactly one of "decide" (makeable from the map), "spec" (question answered), or "one named gap" (one unknown + where to look). A run that stopped with a question passes only if it is one ask (parts allowed) for what the request was missing.

## Observations for the author

1. **The same leak, three times.** In all three maps Angle 1 (the goal number) came back empty. Only run-04 turned that into *the* step (merged with falsifier b: one unknown, one week, one place to look). Runs 01 and 05 both wrote "decide" and then bolted the Angle-1 ask onto the end as a caveat. Pick one: either the decide holds regardless of the number (say so, in one clause), or the step is the gap. The verdict's last sentence is the step; don't let a parenthetical be the real one.

2. **Run-05 argues against itself.** Top-three #2 says positioning A "makes you the third free repo" with the paid feature "already given away" — i.e. A loses whatever the number is. Then the next step says A "wins on reach" if the number is installs. One of those is wrong; the map should not leave the reader to choose.

3. **Run-01's "decide" carries three things out of the room**: engineers on reliability, a Mom-Test + Recall pilot with a two-of-three rule, and the angle-1 homework. The first two are one decision; the third is what fails it. Delete the parenthetical (Angle 1 already records the ask) and run-01 passes.

4. **Run-03 ignored the skill's own shortcut.** Run-02 offers: "The decision is already made and you want the evidence. Say so. The map will still carry everything that contradicts the belief." Run-03's user said exactly that, unprompted — and got a three-part form plus an angle cut instead of the map. Same skill, same missing ground truth, two different front doors; the run-02 packaging ("One ask … four lines") is the one that passes.

5. **RESPONSE.md re-opens doors the verdict shut.** Run-01 ends on "Not done:" with four items; run-04 ends on "Not reached this run" with five; run-05 ends with two asks ("Tell me the number…", "paste five r/ProductManagement threads…"). None are in the MAP verdict, so they do not fail R8 — but the last thing the user reads is a list of gaps after a verdict that named one. If coverage disclosure is wanted, put it above the verdict.
