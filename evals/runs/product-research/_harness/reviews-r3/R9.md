## Observations

1. **Every run passes (a) and (b).** Each has an uncut "Method — the PM canon (never cut)" section naming 3–6 frameworks. The failures are all on (c) and (d), meaning rows where a framework is named but not actually applied.
2. **Continuous Discovery Habits shows up as a name-drop twice (run-02, run-03).** Both cite a Torres blog quote instead of the method. Neither turns the opportunity solution tree into a prediction for this decision. Run-04 is the only one that applies it properly: "spend insights is one solution under an unverified opportunity … cannot rank this solution until churn interviews surface that opportunity".
3. **Kano gets misapplied to positioning headlines (run-02 angle 4, run-05 angle 4 and 5.5).** Both treat "AI skills for PMs" and "evals for PMs" as Kano attributes and infer the class from market supply (free repos, seller count). Kano's evidence is customer satisfaction when a feature is present or absent. Run-01, run-03 and run-04 use it correctly, on product features.
4. **Crossing the Chasm is the most-stretched framework.** Run-01 §8 ("Your 400 accounts are the beachhead") and run-03 §8 ("the 2,000 installed accounts are the beachhead") both call an existing customer base Moore's beachhead. Moore's beachhead is a niche segment a new product dominates to cross from early adopters to pragmatists. Run-02 5.2 has it predicting conversion rate. Run-04 (whole product) and run-05 (pragmatists buy from the leader, so win a niche) describe it correctly.
5. **The "Through X" insight lines are mostly decoration in all runs.** Every angle's insight opens with a framework ("Through *Escaping the Build Trap*", "through Shreyas's LNO", "read as RICE effort"), usually with no prediction and no evidence check. Run-01's RICE line even says the constraints "cut *Effort*" while meaning that effort goes up. A PM would read these as name-drops. I graded only the method sections strictly, so this did not decide any verdict, but it is the main thing that makes a map read as generic research.
6. **Framework sourcing is thin.** JTBD is marked ZERO in run-01 (S30) and run-03 (S42), because only a paywalled HBR header was read. Run-05's SOURCES.md has no framework source at all. Run-01's Kano (S28, Wikipedia quote) and run-01's Mom Test (S29) are the only framework claims backed by an opened source. Descriptions elsewhere come from memory. Most are correct, but they are unverified.
7. **Spot-checks done:** Kano (run-01: correct against S28; run-02/05: misapplied), Mom Test (run-01/04: correct; run-05: misapplied), Obviously Awesome (run-02/04/05: correct, competitive alternatives first; run-03: correct gist, wrong source), RICE (run-04: correct; run-01: direction misworded), Crossing the Chasm (run-04/05: correct; run-01/02/03: stretched), CDH (run-04: correct; run-02/03: not described).
