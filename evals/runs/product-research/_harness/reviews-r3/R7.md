## Observations
- Every run lists all of its *labelled* contradicts rows correctly. Every run fails on the second clause, the unlabelled rows that weaken the belief. The most common way they get past a reviewer: the row is labelled *unknown* or *confirms* even though its own text cuts against the belief (run-02 5.3, run-03 6.3/3.1/3.2, run-04 2.5, run-05 2.2/5.4). The verdict template only collects rows by label, so these slip through.
- "None dropped" / "None was dropped" / "None on B1" / "all of it" are stated in 4 of 5 runs, and each statement is false by this criterion. A completeness claim with no omissions to justify it is the specific dishonesty to target.
- The severity order (worst first): run-04 (B1 "confirmed" while a row shows half the feature is shipped by nobody) > run-05 ("None on B1" against three rows that weaken B1) > run-03 (partial "confirmed" with four omitted complicating rows) > run-02 (one omitted F2 row) > run-01 (omissions are mostly second-order).
- Suggested fix to the skill: in §3, require a sweep of every row whose Finding or Also-changes text includes a weakening word ("not", "no", "only", "undercuts", "wrong", "risk", "unproven", "exists" for an "open slot" belief), not only the Belief column. Ban the phrase "none dropped" unless an explicit dropped-rows list (possibly empty) comes before it.
