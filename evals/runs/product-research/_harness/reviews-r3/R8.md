## Observations

- **run-01, internal contradiction.** Row 1.1 says the Q1 expansion target "decides the next step", and row 1.2 says "If sync accounts are contracting, the 'retention, not expansion' framing breaks". Yet the verdict names a third unknown (substitute share) as the gap. So the map has at least three gaps it says are decisive and picks one without saying why. RESPONSE.md then reopens all of them: "I also need three numbers from you: the Q1 expansion target, whether the QBR mentions were prompted, and the contraction revenue".
- **run-02.** RESPONSE.md turns the bundled baseline into an explicit second ask ("One thing I need from you: roughly how many visitors you expect in week 1, and from where"). The gap can only be closed by shipping both headlines, so the decision the user asked for (which copy ships this week) is actually handed off to an experiment. That fits the "gap" form, but it means no positioning decision is made before launch.
- **run-03, the borderline call.** It passes only because S1 is defined as one ASK row. Under a stricter count, "which metric" is a decision, "baseline" is a lookup, and "target" is a leadership input, which makes three items. The "where to look" (product analytics) only covers the baseline. Row 1.2 (S2, internal demand) is marked as deciding the persona, while Top-three #3 states the persona as settled ("The main user is the stakeholder"), so a second decisive unknown sits in the map without being raised. RESPONSE.md is consistent with the verdict.
- **run-04.** RESPONSE.md adds "Can you pull that number, **and confirm the two apps?**" (row 1.3), which reopens a second ask. It drops the 300-review sample that the MAP had bundled in.
- **run-05.** The "decide" rests on the paid ceiling, which only matters if the goal is free-to-paid conversion. That goal is *drafted and unconfirmed* in section 0 ("The number it is meant to move is not given"). If the user's real goal were signups (the run-02 framing), the verdict's own concession ("Even if A drew more signups") would become the reason to reverse it. The map doesn't flag this, so it passes, but this is where the decide is weakest. RESPONSE.md reopens the goal gap ("Set a target: you didn't give me a baseline or target"), and the map's row 5.4 adds "Run a few real PM conversations before trusting the hero line". Both are pre-launch actions next to a "decide", but neither is said to reverse the decision.
