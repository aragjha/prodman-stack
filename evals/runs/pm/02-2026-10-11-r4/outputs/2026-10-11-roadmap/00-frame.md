# Frame

- **Decision:** Not stated in the request. "Make me a product roadmap" names an artifact, not a
  decision. A roadmap exists to serve a decision (what to build and in what order to hit a goal);
  without that goal, team and time frame, any roadmap produced would be generic and invented.
- **Decider and date:** unknown. Not given in the request.
- **Done looks like:** a product roadmap. Which shape depends on the decision — C01 (idea to
  roadmap) produces an outcome-sequenced plan; C03 turns a feature list into outcomes; F03 reviews
  an existing roadmap. Cannot choose the shape until the decision and inputs are known.
- **Inputs in hand:** none. No product idea, no constraints (team, time frame, goal), no existing
  roadmap, no customer evidence.

- **F03 Roadmap quality review** — if there is an existing roadmap to pressure-test before a review.
