## Attached: interview notes (SYNTHETIC, written for this test)
1. Firm A, 4 seats. "Month end is hell. We chase clients for payment by email, one by one." Asked twice whether reminders could be automatic.
2. Firm B, 11 seats. Happy overall. Wants a dark mode. Mentioned that two clients paid late in September "because they lost the invoice email".

4. Firm D, 2 seats. Considering leaving for a cheaper tool. Main complaint: price per seat. Did not mention reminders.

6. Firm F, 15 seats. Uses a separate tool just to send payment reminders and pays for it. Would drop it if we had reminders built in.
