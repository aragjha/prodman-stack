# 01 · Frame — what to build next quarter

> ALL INPUTS SYNTHETIC. The six interview notes were written for this test. No real customer data.

**Decision:** Pick the single thing to build next quarter for a per-seat B2B invoicing tool (~300 paying firms).
**Decider and date:** The PM (you), at planning in two weeks (~2026-10-25).
**Done looks like:** A one-page decision brief that makes the call, shows the evidence with confidence tags, and states how sure you can be — takeable into the planning meeting.
**Inputs in hand:** Six synthetic interview notes (Firms A–F). No product context file, no churn/support/revenue baseline, no connector data.

**Belief going in (stated so it can be argued against):** Automated payment reminders is the thing.
**What would change the call:** If the business metric that matters is *new-seat growth* rather than retention/ARPU; or if the loudest pain (payment chasing) turns out to affect only a small, non-representative slice of the 300 firms.
