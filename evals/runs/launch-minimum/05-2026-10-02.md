## Verdict
**NOT A LAUNCH** — five of the eight have no evidence behind them and two of those are the named
red flags (a capture that only captures bookers, a payment path no money has ever moved through);
the thing you think is blocking you — the domain — blocks nothing at all.

The work is about **six hours**, not two weeks. But six hours of missing is still missing, and
"basically there" is the phrase this skill exists to delete. Re-run this audit **Monday 2026-10-06**;

**No ✅ was awarded.** Not because the work is bad — several of these are genuinely close — but
because not one item came with a URL, a file, or a transaction. Every line above is an intention.
Item 3 in particular is probably already done; it takes ten minutes to prove it and then it counts.

## The hole, in funnel order
1. **The promise** — 30 min — you. Everything downstream inherits the vagueness. A capture form for
   an unclear promise collects nothing; proof of "fixing ops" is unprovable.
2. **The artifact** — 2 hrs — you. Run the draft on a real founder once. You cannot sell a thing you
   have not watched someone use.
3. **The surface** — 10 min — you. Verify the staging URL loads for a stranger. Then stop thinking
   about the domain.
4. **The capture** — 1 hr — you. This is the expensive hole. Every post, intro and DM you send until
   this exists spends its traffic once and keeps nothing.
5. **The proof** — 1 hr — you + one founder friend. One number, not a quote.
6. **The path to pay** — 45 min — you. A test invoice to yourself. Discovering a wrong account
   number mid-deal costs the deal.
7. **The room** — 15 min — you. One line of copy.
8. **The signal** — 10 min — you. Written before launch or it does not count.

Fixing the domain first would be fixing the far end of a leak. Fixing the capture first is the one
that stops you losing people you have already earned.

## AFTER (not launch blockers)
- **The domain transfer.** Improves none of the eight. A staging URL that loads is a surface; a nicer
  hostname is a preference. Point it at the page whenever it clears — launch does not wait on it.
- **The last 20% of the diagnostic doc.** Improves item 2 only after item 2 is already satisfied by
  using it once.
- **Written testimonials from both friends.** Item 5 does not accept testimonials, so these improve
  nothing on the list. Collect them later for the page; collect the one *number* now.
- **Anything else you were about to add to the page.** If you cannot name which of the eight it
  improves, it goes here.

## The signal
We will know it worked when **invoices sent for this offer** reaches **1** by **2026-10-31**.

Leading indicator, checked weekly, not a substitute: **booked diagnostic calls**. If that number is
zero on 2026-10-13, the problem is distribution, not the offer — and you will be able to tell,
because the capture will be there to show you how many people came and did not book.
