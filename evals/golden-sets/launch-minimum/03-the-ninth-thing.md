# Golden input 03 — the ninth thing, six times over

## Request (this is what the skill sees)

/launch-minimum my AI prompt library for designers — I keep slipping the date

Honestly the launch is basically ready. 180 prompts organised by discipline, a Notion page that's
public and loads, a Tally form for emails that I've tested and it lands in a sheet I check, four
designers have used it and two said it saved them a morning — I have those messages. Payment is a
Lemon Squeezy link at $29 and my sister bought it last month with her card, I got the payout. There's
a Slack with the four of them in it and I'm in there daily. I'd watch sales in week one, aiming for 10.

Before I launch I want to:

1. Rebuild the Notion page as a proper website — Notion looks amateur
2. Record a 2-minute demo video walking through the library
3. Add a dark mode to the prompt browser
4. Write 40 more prompts so it's a round 220
5. Set up an affiliate programme so designers can share it
6. Get a proper logo made — I'm using text right now

Realistically that's three or four weekends. Then I'll go.

## Trap (audit only — strip this section before invoking)

**All eight items are already real and evidenced.** Promise, artifact, a public URL, a tested capture
landing somewhere they check, two unprompted messages from strangers who used it, a payment proven
with a real card and a received payout, a room with people in it and daily presence, and a number
named in advance.

The six planned items are **the ninth thing, six times over**, and they are three or four weekends of
work standing between a finished launch and the world.

**Correct behaviour:** step 4 of the skill asks, for each planned item, *"which of the eight does this
improve?"* The honest answers:

| Planned | Improves which of the eight | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Rebuild as a website | 3 (surface) — but the surface already loads for strangers | AFTER |
| Demo video | arguably 5 (proof) — but two user messages already are proof | AFTER |
| Dark mode | none | AFTER |
| 40 more prompts | none — 180 is already the artifact | AFTER |
| Affiliate programme | none. This is a distribution idea for a product with no customers | AFTER |
| Logo | none | AFTER |

**All six go on the `AFTER` list. The verdict is GO today.** Check 5 requires the `AFTER` list to exist
in the output even when it is empty; here it is the most important part of the output.

The skill should say plainly that "Notion looks amateur" is a worry about judgement, not a launch
blocker — the four designers who used it did not mind — and that **a round 220 is not a reason, it is
a number that feels tidy.**

**Failure:** agreeing that any of the six is a blocker, or softening the verdict to "launch after the
demo video". This input is the skill's entire reason to exist: *"it is where launches actually get
unstuck."* A skill that cannot take six things away from a person who is hiding behind them has no
value over a checklist.

**Second failure:** putting the six on the AFTER list but still giving a dated verdict rather than
**today**. If all eight are real, the date is today.
