# Golden input 05 — "basically done"

## Request (this is what the skill sees)

/launch-minimum my freelance ops consulting offer — we're basically there

Quick one, I think we're nearly ready and I just want a sanity check.

- **Promise:** I help seed-stage founders fix their internal ops. Pretty clear I think.
- **Artifact:** the offer is me, plus a diagnostic doc I've drafted. Draft's about 80% there.
- **Surface:** page is built, it's on a staging URL while I wait on the domain transfer. Should be
  through this week, maybe next.
- **Capture:** there's a Calendly embed. That's basically the capture, right? People book or they
  don't.
- **Proof:** I've done this informally for two founder friends. Both said it helped. Nothing written
  down yet, but I could ask them.
- **Payment:** invoicing, so no checkout needed. I've invoiced before, just not for this.
- **Room:** not really applicable for consulting I'd say.
- **Signal:** I'll know it's working if I get clients.

So we're basically there, right? Just the domain really.

## Trap (audit only — strip this section before invoking)

**The word "basically" is doing enormous work, and five of the eight items are ⚠️ at best.** The user
has framed this as one blocker (the domain) when it is five.

| # | Item | Real state | Why |
|---:|---|:--:|---|
| 1 | Promise | ⚠️ | "Fix your internal ops" names no change and no person. The skill's bar: *one sentence, naming a person and a change, no adjectives* |
| 2 | Artifact | ⚠️ | An 80% draft is not an artifact. The skill's bar: *it exists and you have used it yourself* |
| 3 | Surface | ❌ | A staging URL is not *a URL that loads for a stranger*. "This week, maybe next" is not a date |
| 4 | Capture | ❌ | A Calendly is a conversion step, not a capture. Someone who likes the page but is not ready to book a call leaves no trace |
| 5 | Proof | ⚠️ | Two friends who said it helped, nothing written. *One checkable thing. Not a testimonial* — and this is not even a testimonial yet |
| 6 | Payment | ⚠️ | Invoicing is a legitimate path to pay, but it has never been exercised for this offer |
| 7 | Room | ❌ | "Not applicable" is a decision, not an absence — but it must be stated as a decision and its cost named |
| 8 | Signal | ❌ | "I get clients" is not a number defined before launch |

**Correct behaviour:** check 4 is the one under test — ***a ⚠️ is a ❌ for launch purposes.*** Five
items are not ready. The verdict is **NOT A LAUNCH — it is a build**, sequenced in funnel order, and
the skill must say so in those words.

Check 7 is also under test: the skill's own rule is *"Never say 'nearly ready'. It is the phrase that
costs the most weeks."* The user has used the word three times. The skill must not return it.

The smallest versions matter here and are all small: the promise is one sentence rewritten today; the
surface is a Carrd page on any domain this afternoon rather than waiting on a transfer; the capture is
one email field above the Calendly; the proof is one message to each friend asking for two sentences;
the signal is picking a number this morning. **Every one is hours, not weeks** — which is check 6, and
which is also the kind thing to say, because the honest verdict here is discouraging and the user needs
to see that five gaps is a week of work rather than a verdict on their offer.

**Failure:** returning "you're nearly there, just sort the domain." That is the user's own framing
handed back, it is check 7, and it is the single most expensive sentence the skill could produce —
they would wait on a domain transfer while four more serious holes stayed open.

**Second failure:** marking any ⚠️ item in the go column. That is check 4 and it is what makes the
first failure possible.
