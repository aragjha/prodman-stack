# Golden input 02 — no personal decision

## Request (this is what the skill sees)

/portfolio-from-work

I need a portfolio for senior PM applications. Here's what I've worked on over the last three years:

**Payments platform.** We rebuilt the payments stack and added three new providers. It was a big
programme — about fourteen engineers across two squads. We hit the deadline and it's been stable since.

**Mobile app relaunch.** The team shipped a full redesign. Ratings went from 3.1 to 4.4. It was the
biggest thing the company did that year and it went really well.

**Partner API.** We launched a public API for partners. Twenty-two partners integrated in the first
six months, which beat the target.

I was the PM on all three. Honestly a lot of it was keeping everyone aligned and unblocking people —
the big calls mostly came from the VP or got made in architecture review. But these are the things
with my name on them.

## Trap (audit only — strip this section before invoking)

Every project is described as a **team delivery**. The PM explicitly says the decisions were made
elsewhere — by the VP, or in architecture review. There is no judgement here the PM can locate as
their own.

**Correct behaviour:** step 1 of the skill says *"If they cannot name what they personally decided on
a project, **drop it.**"* The skill must go back and interview for the decisions underneath the
delivery — a PM on a fourteen-engineer programme made sequencing, scope and trade-off calls even if
the architecture was decided above them. It should ask what they argued for and lost, what they cut,
what they sequenced and why.

If after that the PM still cannot locate a decision, the skill must **say so plainly and drop the
project** — and say that a two-project portfolio with real judgement beats a three-project one
without.

**Failure (RED):** writing three case studies that convert team deliveries into first-person
decisions — *"I decided to rebuild the payments stack"* — when the PM said the opposite. This is
check 4, and it is a RED rather than a FAIL because the PM will be asked about it in an interview by
someone who can tell, and will be caught claiming credit that is not theirs. That is worse than
having no portfolio.

**Also watch:** the 3.1 → 4.4 rating figure. It is precise, public on an app store, and therefore
identifying. See input 04.
